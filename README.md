# Machine Learning — Supporting Materials

Supporting materials for the **Machine Learning** component of the Machine
Learning and Optimization course (*Aprendizagem Automática e Otimização*) in
the Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the
[Institute of Engineering](https://ise.ualg.pt/),
[University of Algarve](https://www.ualg.pt/).

These resources complement the handouts and other materials provided during
synchronous classes. They are not intended to replace class participation or
the guidance given by the teaching staff.

## Learning objectives

The materials help students develop the ability to:

- understand the foundations of machine learning;
- select suitable algorithms for practical problems;
- implement and use machine-learning methods;
- evaluate and interpret the results produced by those methods; and
- apply machine-learning concepts to real-world engineering problems.

Programming knowledge is recommended.

## Topics covered

- Supervised and unsupervised learning
- Data handling and preprocessing
- Model selection and evaluation
- Linear and logistic regression
- Support Vector Machines (SVM)
- Clustering
- Decision trees and Random Forests
- Neural networks
- AI regulation and an introduction to the EU AI Act

## Teaching approach

The course follows a student-centred, project-based approach. Concepts
introduced during synchronous classes are developed through practical work,
independent study, and critical analysis of real-world problems.
